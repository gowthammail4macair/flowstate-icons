# Artwork distribution record

This first release is private. Original FlowState contributions retain MIT OR Apache-2.0. Vendor artwork and marks retain their respective rights. The included source notices identify the exact collections and conversions. The public availability of a source does not establish permission to publish a standalone mirror.

| Collection | Public redistribution review |
|---|---|
| Official AWS and AWS-derived stencils | Diagram use documented. Complete standalone library redistribution requires further review. |
| Azure, Microsoft historical sets, Google Cloud and Firebase | Review each collection's permitted uses, transforms, provenance and scope for complete library distribution. |
| Cisco-derived shapes | Verify the historical source and conversion against the vendor's guidance. |
| draw.io | Keep version-specific notices, adaptation details and original vendor restrictions. |
| Devicon and Lucide | Retain copyright/license notices and review applicable marks. |

See the notices beside the artwork and the source record in docs/licensing.md. Maintain private visibility until a separate publication decision.
